Unit 1 Notes:

Outcomes:
1) Understand the issues and challenges associated with managing tools and strategies.
2) Become familiar with the security data requirements.

Unit 5 Notes:

Outcomes:
1) Be able to explain data cleaning, using examples.
2) Explain how automation must be clear and focused on the required task.
3) Understand the inputs and outputs.
4) Explain the use of tools for automation.

Unit 6 Notes:

Outcomes:
1) Discuss the construction and terminology associated with creating a database.
2) Identify technical terminology used in the construction of a relational database.
3) Be familiar with the process involved in creating a logical database design.
4) Evaluate the requirements for creating a physical architecture database design.
5) Analyse and evaluate the requirements and limitations for creating a physical design.
6) Understand the fundamentals of normalisation.

This unit explored the database design and normalisation. I found the concepts of keys challenging to understand and needed to do my own research to better my understanding. The lecturecast provided a decent overview of the concepts that need to be understood. This unit also required the submission of the first assignment, which was the group project as well as the peer review. The submission was smooth and the peer review provided a chance to reflect on the team work aspect of the submission.
