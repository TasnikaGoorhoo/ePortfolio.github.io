Element Initial Project Proposal (Unit 6) Final Project (Unit 11)
Project Objectives
  • Critically analyse data wrangling problems and determine appropriate methodologies, tools, and techniques (involving preparing, cleaning, exploring, creating, optimising and evaluating big data) to solve them.
  • Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment, adopting real life perspectives on team roles and organisation.
  • Design, develop and evaluate solutions for processing datasets and solving complex problems in various environments using relevant programming paradigms.
  • Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment, adopting real life perspectives on team roles and organisation.
Scope and Focus Working as a group of consultants/developers (pre-determined), we had to design and build a logical database. Chosen real-world example of a retail store with data readily available. Delivery of a design report was required. Working individually, based on the design proposal, write an executive summary on the full report of the completed design and logical build of a logical database.
Methodology and Techniques Working through the module material and our own research. Regular group calls to feedback on each person's section of work and meet project milestones. Working through the assignment requirements thoroughly and carrying out comprehensive research as well as engaging in seminars to ensure understanding.
Sources and Resources Module material, external research and material, own knowledge as a group Module material, external resources such as research papers, textbooks and journals.
Challenges and Limitations
  • Ensuring consistency throughout the report.
  • Trusting each team member to carry out comprehensive research for their respective sections and ensure the questions are answered fully.
  • Establishing workflows as a team and being mindful of communication methods.
  • Time management was critical as I had to write the full report on my own.
  • Work on grasping the details and applying it in the retail example chosen ensuring the nuances are accounted for.
Outcomes and Results Feedback was that the report did not meet all the requirements. The critical analysis was not suffice with limited references and was missing the required diagram. Report was well written but needed more alignment to the assignment requirements. The report was comprehensive in meeting the requirements, good analysis and understanding of work and concepts. Data privacy section required more work and some structural gaps in the report such as linking referenced figures and tables.
Learning and Development As a group, we could have worked through each assignment requirement together and discussed it more.
Carrying out a more comprehensive critical analysis follows a process including enough references.
Personally, this was a chance for me to show up as a better leader.		 Individually, I practiced my time management skills and had onus on the rate of the assignment and meeting the deadline.
Reflection on Collaboration vs. Individual Work This assignment provided the opportunity to enhance understanding of foundational knowledge and to work as a team to deliver a successful report. This assignment provided the opportunity to enhance my technical knowledge, engage deeper with the material and reinforce importance of individual accountability in successful delivery.
