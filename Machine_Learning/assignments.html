Assignment Notes

Element Development Team Project: Project Report & Peer Review (Unit 6) Development Individual Project: Presentation (Unit 11)
Project Objectives • Understand the applicability and challenges associated with different datasets for the use of machine learning algorithms.
• Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment, adopting real-life perspectives on team roles and organisation.		 • Apply and critically appraise machine learning techniques to real-world problems, particularly where technical risk and uncertainty is involved.
Scope and Focus Working as a group of developers (pre-determined), we had to carry out analysis on Airbnb data using regression and clustering. Two business related questions were decided on. Delivery of a report was required. Working individually, based on the CIFAR-10 dataset, based on the deep learning track, train, evaluate, analyse and discuss through a recorded presentation.
Methodology and Techniques Working through the module material and our own research. Regular group meetings to feedback on each person's section of work and meet project milestones. A group contract was signed. Working through the assignment requirements thoroughly and carrying out comprehensive research as well as engaging in seminars to ensure understanding.
Sources and Resources Module material, external research and material, own knowledge as a group Module material, external resources such as research papers, textbooks and journals.
Challenges and Limitations • Ensuring consistency throughout the report writing.
• Trusting each team member to carry out comprehensive research for their respective sections.
• Establishing workflows as a team and being mindful of communication methods.
• Ensuring effective teamwork to deliver on time.		 • Time management was critical as I had to carry out all work on my own and record the presentation.
• Work on grasping the concepts and applying it.
Outcomes and Results Feedback was that is was a strong report, showing technical competence, clear structure and relevant insights for Airbnb's strategy. Not received at time of writing.
Learning and Development As a group, a stronger and more comprehensive critical interrogation of model limitations, clearer quantification of the business impacts and tighter integration of visuals into the report narrative. Individually, I practiced my time management skills and had onus on the rate of the assignment and meeting the deadline.
Reflection on Collaboration vs. Individual Work This assignment provided the opportunity to enhance understanding of foundational knowledge and to work as a team to deliver a successful report. It allowed to improve technical and teamwork skills. This assignment provided the opportunity to enhance my technical knowledge, engage deeper with the material and reinforce importance of individual accountability.
