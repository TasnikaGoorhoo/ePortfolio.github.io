Element Literature Review (Unit 7) Research Proposal Presentation (Unit 10)
Project Objectives Undertake critical evaluation of existing literature and create and produce a standalone literature review on the chosen topic. Evaluate literature and research design/methodology/data analyses and create and critically evaluate a research proposal on the topic selected. Delivered the proposal in a narrated presentation.
Scope and Focus Topic chosen from list in unit 1. Focus on current knowledge on the topic area. Focus on planning a research project including importance, research question, aims/objectives, methodology, ethics/risks, challenges/gaps, data analysis, artefacts, timeline. Presented in slide format, in a clear narrative presentation.
Methodology and Techniques Creating a scope of the literature to be reviewed. Searching literature. Critical reading, thematic synthesis, structure review and academic writing. Creating a proposal structure 'golden thread' and follow the guideline of creating each element of the proposal. Problem → research question → aims/objectives → literature → methodology → data analysis plan → ethics/risks → artefacts → timeline; slide and transcript and presentation design
Sources and Resources Relevant, current literature of importance to demonstrate coverage. Maintain correct referencing and integrity. Critically evaluate key literature and appropriate references in slides and transcript. Use of visual tools for slide creation. Recording of the presentation.
Challenges and Limitations Perfectionism tendencies and stuck in loop of reading. Difficulty synthesizing and comparing studies critically. Ensuring strong integrity. Time constraint for the presentation and ensuring all points included. Need to balance slide elements and transcript for each slide. Ensuring all essential parts of a proposal is included.
Outcomes and Results A well-structured literature review
1. Summarize current knowledge
2. Demonstrate awareness of relevant literature
3. Compare studies showing similar/contrasting views
4. Research and writing		 A well-presented, well audible, presentation of the research proposal with a transcript. All sections of a proposal included. Ensuring all referencing guidelines adhered to.
Learning and Development Improving skills in academic investigation, critical analysis, academic writing and referencing. Improving skills in research planning, methodology justification, ethics exploration requirement, professional presentation skills
Reflection on Collaboration vs. Individual Work Individual work. Obtained tutor feedback on the outline which aided in ensuring the right path was followed. Individual work. Obtained feedback from tutor on the proposal outline which helped provide guidance.
